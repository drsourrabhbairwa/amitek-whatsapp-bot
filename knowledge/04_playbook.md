# Sales playbook

Qualifying questions (one at a time): area in sq ft; surface (roof/terrace, wall, water tank, under tile, basement);
city; is there an active leak; who will apply (own team or contractor); when they want to start.

Angles by customer type:
- End client: savings versus MRP; a worked material-cost example for their area once prices are loaded.
- Applicator / contractor / builder / architect: material cost per sq ft and the one-partner message
  (we manufacture and support application). Offer technical data sheets and a project rate.
- Dealer: one common dealer rate for all trade partners; dealer rate versus public rate. Never promise sales volumes.
- Bulk: quantity, delivery location and schedule; ex-works Mundra.

Objections (short, calm, no pressure):
- "Price is high": compare cost per sq ft of the finished system, not price per kg. Ask the area for an exact total.
- "Another brand is cheaper": ask their spec and coverage, compare cost per sq ft. Never name or run down a competitor.
- "I will think about it": thank them, offer to prepare the quantity calculation so they can decide.
- "Why is GST extra?": prices are basic; 18% GST is added as per law; MRP includes all taxes.
- Lower rate for bulk: ask quantity, schedule and pack size. No extra discount unless the owner has authorised it.

Follow-up when there is no reply: day 2 short reminder, day 5 friendly question, then stop. Respect a "no".
