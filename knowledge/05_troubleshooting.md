# Basic troubleshooting (safe, general facts only — not a substitute for a site visit)

Use this only to show genuine interest and give one quick, safe fact when a customer describes a problem. Never give
a step-by-step DIY fix, never promise a result, and always still hand off for an actual site visit or quotation.

- **Roof/terrace leak after rain**: usually surface cracks, pooling water (bad slope/drainage), or an old waterproofing
  coat that has worn out. Common fix category: a crack-bridging coat such as *Smart Duo Shield* over a prepared surface.
- **Wall seepage / damp patch**: often comes from a crack, a gap at a window/pipe joint, or water travelling from a
  roof/terrace above rather than the wall itself. Needs the source checked, not just the visible patch.
- **Water tank leak or seepage**: usually a construction joint or crack below the waterline. Needs a cementitious
  system applied from the water-facing side, such as *HMC-501*.
- **Bathroom / under-tile leak into the room below**: almost always the waterproofing under the tile has failed or
  was never done before tiling. Needs *HMC-501* under new tiling, not a surface patch over old tiles.
- **Basement / underground seepage**: usually ground water pressure finding a crack or joint; needs a system rated
  for negative/positive water pressure, decided after seeing the site.
- **Blistering or peeling of an old coating**: usually moisture trapped under the old coat, or it was applied on a
  damp/dirty surface. The old coat needs removing before reapplying.
- **Ponding water that never fully dries**: a slope/drainage problem first; a coating alone will not fix standing
  water, the slope needs correcting too.

Always end with: this is a general reason, not a diagnosis — the team can confirm after seeing a photo or visiting
the site. Never claim a guaranteed fix, timeline, or price here.
