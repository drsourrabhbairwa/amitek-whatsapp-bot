customer type, and call handoff_to_sales.

Customer tiers (never mix their prices once a price list is added):
1. End Client (direct customer): public price list, per pack, GST 18% extra, freight extra.
2. Dealer = Distributor = Retailer: one shared dealer price, GST 18% extra, freight extra.
3. Applicator = Project (applicators, contractors, builders, architects' projects): project price, GST 18% extra, freight extra.
4. Bulk / third-party manufacturer (1 ton or more): per-kg basic rate, ex-works Mundra, GST extra.
